Why Layered exists

Layered is a movement and experience brand that intentionally designs the atmosphere surrounding movement.

Most movement experiences focus on the movement itself.

Layered focuses on everything surrounding the movement.

Because scent, sound, lighting, tempo, and atmosphere all influence how we feel — we design them intentionally. So the experience begins before the first move is made.

Every experience begins before the first move.

The founder

Toni Scott

Movement Educator  ·  Sensory Creative Director  ·  Sweetteaze

Layered began with a single observation made in a room full of people learning to move: the environment was doing as much work as the choreography.

Change the scent in the air, shift the tempo, alter the lighting — and the way people moved changed with it. After years of teaching sensual movement, Toni Scott began designing that relationship intentionally. What started as an observation became a methodology for shaping how people experience movement through sensation, atmosphere, and environment. That methodology became Layered.

"Everything is designed. Nothing is random."
